Sales Data Analysis Dashboard
1. Project Title / Headline

Sales Performance Dashboard: Revenue, Profit, Product & Shipping Insights (2024-2025)

Alternative: Interactive Sales Analytics Dashboard in Power BI: From Raw Orders to Business Decisions

2. Short Description / Purpose

An interactive dashboard that consolidates two years of sales data (2024 and 2025) into a single view for tracking revenue, profitability, product performance, regional demand, and order fulfilment efficiency. It helps sales and operations teams see which products, regions, and customer segments drive results, and where profit or shipping performance needs attention. A year slicer lets users compare periods instantly.

3. Tech Stack

Power BI Desktop: dashboard design, slicers, and interactive visuals
DAX: measures such as Sum of Sales Revenue, Sum of Profit, Average Profit, and Average Days to Ship
Power Query: data cleaning and transformation
Excel / CSV: source data files

4. Data Resource
   
Scope: transactional sales records for 2024 and 2025
Key fields: Order Date, Product Name, Region, Customer Segment, Order Priority, Sales Revenue, Profit, Shipping Days

5. Features / Highlights

Interactive elements

Year slicer (2024 / 2025) that filters every visual at once
Cross-filtering across charts for drill-down analysis

Visuals and insights

Total sales revenue of about ₹22.93 lakh, broken down by product in a sortable table with totals.
Product concentration: the Business Laptop i7 alone contributes about 28% of revenue. With the Wooden Conference Table and UltraWide Monitor, the top 3 products account for about 59%. Stationery items (paper, binders) contribute very little.
Regional performance: South leads (about 0.68M) and North trails (about 0.42M), which points to an expansion opportunity in the North.
Segment profitability: Consumer generates the most profit (38.4%), followed by Corporate (33.6%) and Small Business (28%).
Monthly trends: area charts track revenue and profit month by month with headline KPIs (194.67K revenue, 79.95K profit). The average-profit bar chart ranks months by profitability.
Fulfilment efficiency: average shipping time is about 3.35 days, and it is nearly identical across Critical, High, Medium, and Low priority orders. Critical orders are not shipped faster, which suggests a process improvement opportunity.
Design choices
Consistent red colour theme for readability
Clean layout with KPI cards, trends, and breakdowns in a logical flow
